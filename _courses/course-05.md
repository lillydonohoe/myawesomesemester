## About this course

In this course I will be learning about African American history. Specifically the culture, identify, and experiences of African Americans. I will be diving deeper into slavery, abolition, reconstruction, Jim Crow, and different movements. I will be learning this information through readings, music, and films. I am taking this course because I want to learn more about different cultures and history. I am intrigued to see how music, film and other visual tools of communication can tell such strong stories.  

## About Professor Onwubiko Agozino

Dr. Onwubiko Agozino is a sociology and African Studies professor here at Virginia Tech. He got his bachelors degree from a University in Nigeria and his masters from University of Cambridge. He taught at several universities before coming to VT in 2009 to teach African Studies. Virginia Tech has referred to him as a scholar-activist, he connects his real social issues super well with his academic research. He has a lot of knowledge in order to teach this course, and I am excited to learn more from him. 

### Research interests

He has very defined research interests. He is very direct on learning about African and African American studies. As well as different race, class, and gender studies. A big part of his research focusses on how criminal justice systems have affected people of African decent over the years. He takes what he has learned in his education, and teaches that to his students in an efficient way. 

### Publications

He has published many works over the years that all connect crime, justice, race and different experiences. These are a few of his publications he has been a part of: 

- Black Women and the Criminal Justice System
- Counter-Colonial Criminology: A Critique of Imperialist Reason
- Pan African Issues in Crime and Justice
- Critical, Creative and Centered Scholar-Activism
- Routledge Handbook of Africana Criminologies
- Community Policing in Nigeria: Rationale, Principles, and Practice

### Service and outreach

Community engagement is an important part of his academic work. In the past he has served as the Director of African Studies at Virginia Tech. He created a Community Policing in Nigeria project that wasn't solely for academic research, but to promote conversations about policing, and violence in Nigeria. He is very involved and is very proud of the work he has accomplished.
